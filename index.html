Prices are Google Flights Explore snapshots (round trip, 1 traveler, economy, CAD) captured on the "Last updated" date — they can change or disappear at any time. "Below usual" is Google's Price insights figure. "Lowest seen" marks the cheapest sighting of that destination since tracking began. Status is set at each morning's check from the trip's departure date: a deal whose departure has passed since it was last checked shows as Expired, one leaving within 7 days as Ending soon, otherwise Active; the four rows from the first day of tracking (Oct 15, 2026) have no captured dates and show as Unknown. Starred deals are saved only in this browser. Map: OpenStreetMap contributors, via Leaflet. Points of interest (in the map's layers menu) load live from OpenStreetMap data via the Overpass API, around the destination you pick or the current map view. Map labels are in English by default (Esri street map — a few places, like Japan, print the local name alongside the English one); the layers menu also offers the local-language OpenStreetMap and satellite views. Drops use Skyscanner's published rule: a fare is a Drop when its newest sighting is at least 20% below the lowest sighting of the same destination and travel dates in the previous 7 days; the struck-through price is that 7-day low. Price snapshots (Google Flights and Skyscanner) have been recorded daily since Nov 4, 2026, so the 7-day windows fill in day by day — a route can only show as a Drop once it has been sighted on at least two different days. Skyscanner's own Drops feed is app-only and tracks individual providers hourly, so its list will not match this one exactly; the rule, the threshold and the sorting (biggest drop % first) are the same.
